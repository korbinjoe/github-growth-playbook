## Growth interpretation

Inference: a usable client can turn attention to a model into a first hands-on experience. Clear contribution tasks and recognition can help a small team sustain maintenance after the influx. Neither the interview summary nor the program establishes a causal breakdown of growth.

## Reusable actions

- Prepare an easy first-use guide for a concrete model or workflow.
- Turn recurring problems into public contribution tasks with clear recognition.
- Track first successful use and retained contributors alongside the volume of incoming attention.

## Unresolved questions

- Which posts drove the DeepSeek-period influx, and what fraction of newcomers stayed?
- How did contributor incentives affect completed fixes and ongoing participation?

These actions are recommendations, not independently measured channel effects. The original 2026-10-10 snapshot is retained; totals do not represent two-year additions or users.
