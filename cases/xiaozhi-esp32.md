## Growth interpretation

Inference: a talking device is a visible result that hardware vendors, makers, and educators can each demonstrate. Examples and board compatibility can make those partners ongoing distribution participants. Cooperation is documented; referral volume and the initial trigger are not.

## Reusable actions

- Show the working object, the required components, and the shortest reproduction route.
- Publish compatibility notes and starter materials for tutorial authors.
- Record partner introductions, completed builds, and continued device use separately.

## Unresolved questions

- Which original demo or tutorial triggered the first broad influx?
- How many referred viewers completed a build and kept using it?

These actions are recommendations, not independently measured channel effects. The original 2026-10-10 snapshot is retained; totals do not represent two-year additions or users.
