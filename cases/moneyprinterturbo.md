## Growth interpretation

Inference: a finished video is easier to explain than a list of model capabilities. Tutorials can demonstrate an entire task, while configurable stages give developers reasons to adapt the code. The retrospective is evidence of the author’s interpretation, not a measured account of the initial growth trigger.

## Reusable actions

- Market the completed task with a reproducible input/output example.
- Give tutorial authors a short route to a usable result and show the intermediate stages users can change.
- Measure successful renders, retained use, and output quality; forks do not prove deployments and stars do not prove video performance.

## Unresolved questions

- Which original posts or tutorials drove the first sustained influx?
- Which users successfully produced and reused videos, and what share of attention became actual usage?
