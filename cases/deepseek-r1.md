## Growth interpretation

Inference: model access lets users try it, evaluation tables invite comparisons, and a report gives researchers and editors material to discuss. Coordinating those artifacts can lower the cost of third-party explanation. Their existence does not isolate the marketing contribution from technical performance and broader attention.

## Reusable actions

- Prepare an accessible trial or artifact, evaluation method, and technical explanation together.
- Give different audiences materials appropriate to their tasks.
- Separate publisher evaluations, independent reproductions, and adoption measurements.

## Unresolved questions

- Which release materials led to successful trials and repeat adoption?
- What distribution practices remain effective at an individual developer’s budget and scale?
