## Inference

The upstream product supplied familiarity; model choice, configuration, and cost supplied a concrete need. Users could understand what a supporting tool improved.

Months of iteration separated early exploration from concentrated coverage. Ongoing explanations and updates in the comments also reveal demand and usability issues.

## Reusable actions

- Find one concrete obstacle in a growing ecosystem.
- Demonstrate improvements through familiar operations.
- Align the name, introduction, and tutorials with existing user needs.
- Show work early, respond to feedback, and improve first use.
