## Growth interpretation

Inference: local data, model access, examples, and usable outputs can give an upstream project a new audience. Installation guides and report demonstrations make the adaptation explainable. The evidence supports meaningful workflow localization, but it does not establish the initial viral channel or the accuracy of generated financial research.

## Reusable actions

- Credit upstream and explain exactly which workflow gaps the adaptation fills.
- Demonstrate reproducible local examples and usable report outputs.
- Explain setup, data dependencies, output limitations, and component licensing; judge growth by completed research tasks, not claimed investment returns.
