## Growth interpretation

Inference: comparing the original and revised prompt on the same task makes improvement inspectable. Immediate access can reduce discovery friction; saved templates and prompt assets can encourage repeated use. Distribution routes are documented, but their traffic contribution and quality uplift have not been independently measured.

## Reusable actions

- Demonstrate a specific task with comparable inputs and evaluation criteria.
- Put trial access beside the example and explain model setup and limitations.
- Publish reusable templates and guides for concrete use cases instead of only claiming better prompts.

## Unresolved questions

- What was the first launch channel and the strongest external handoff?
- How many visitors connected a model, completed a comparison, saved a template, and returned?

These actions are recommendations, not independently measured channel effects. The original 2026-10-10 snapshot is retained; totals do not represent two-year additions or users.
