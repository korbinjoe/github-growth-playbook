## Growth interpretation

Inference: Claude Code and Codex already supply an audience and recognizable terminology. A graphical demonstration can show a concrete improvement—switching providers without manual configuration edits. Installers and reliable updates reduce the gap between a recommendation and repeat use. The sequence supports community discovery and usability improvements; it does not identify which action produced the most stars. Sponsor visibility may give providers a reason to introduce the tool, but paid acquisition and sponsor contribution remain unverified.
